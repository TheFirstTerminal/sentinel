Privacy

Short version: Sentinel runs on your own server and never sends us anything. This site has no cookies, no analytics and no ads. We only keep what we need to sell you Pro. Last updated 26 November 2026.

Sentinel itself

Sentinel Free and Pro run on your server. Your traffic, logs, dashboard accounts and settings stay there. Sentinel has no telemetry and doesn't contact us. A Pro license key is checked offline, on your server. Sentinel only connects to other places if you set it up to, such as your alert webhook, Telegram, syslog or OpenTelemetry collector, or Let's Encrypt for automatic HTTPS.

This website

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Like any web host, GitHub sees your IP address when you visit; see GitHub's privacy statement. The download page asks GitHub's public API for the latest Free release.

When you paste a Pro license key on the download page, it goes to dl.tft.markets, a Cloudflare Worker we run. The key is checked and used to make download links. It isn't stored, and we don't keep logs of it. Cloudflare handles that connection; see Cloudflare's privacy policy.

When you buy Pro

We keep a record of each sale. It holds the company name, the email address or WhatsApp number you contacted us from, the plan, the number of servers, the dates, and the license key, which also contains the name and email. We also keep our messages with you and the proof of payment you send. We use this only to give you your key, renew it, answer support questions and keep the accounts we're required to keep. We don't sell it or share it, except with the payment service you chose (Qi Card or Zain Cash), which has its own records of the transfer.

We keep sales records for as long as you are a customer, then for as long as accounting law requires. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, unless we're required to keep it, by writing to support@tft.markets.
